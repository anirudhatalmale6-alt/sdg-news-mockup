How we handle paid content

A publication covering the Global Goals only has value if readers trust it. So the rules are simple, published, and do not bend for a buyer:

  • Every paid placement is visibly labelled as advertising or sponsored content.
  • Sponsors have no input into editorial coverage, including coverage about themselves.
  • Sponsored articles are excluded from the news feed rankings and from “most read”.
  • We publish who our sponsors are.
