Logo — the problem, and three compliant directions

The supplied file places the name inside the official UN SDG colour wheel. The UN's published guidelines list that exact treatment under "DO NOT". Everything below avoids the wheel entirely while keeping the goal palette.
As suppliedCannot be used
UN guidelines, page 21: "DO NOT place entity's logo inside the colour wheel" and "DO NOT place a color in the middle of SDG colour wheel".
Section II: "The integration or combination of any element of the SDG Logo and/or any of the 17 SDG icons into a separate logo design is prohibited."
Separately, an advertising-funded site is a commercial use, which needs prior written UN permission and a licence agreement.
